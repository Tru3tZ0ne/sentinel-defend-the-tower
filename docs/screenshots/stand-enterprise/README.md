# Enterprise campaign visual review

Captured from `stand-enterprise-balance-v1` with Playwright at desktop 1440x1000
and phone 390x844. These images are development evidence, not runtime assets.

- `xdr-charge-FIXTURE.png`: real charge transition with all ten product types.
- `xdr-fire-FIXTURE.png`: the main beam fires from XDR and its mark remains clear.
- `mobile-xdr-FIXTURE.png`: compact equipment rail, score strip above the action,
  official XDR mark/name and unobstructed lower movement area.
- `failure-FIXTURE.png`: genuine deadline failure, with no ranking submission.
- `result-FIXTURE.png`, `result-zh-FIXTURE.png`: actual final-kill callback and
  weighted result/ranking in both languages, with seeded component work.
- `menu-zh.png`: localized campaign instructions.

Every `FIXTURE` capture deliberately stages equipment, targets, timing and/or
accumulated work for inspection. It does not prove a natural playthrough,
human win rate or hardware frame rate. Balance and rendering measurements are
documented in `../../STAND_ENTERPRISE_BALANCE.md`.
