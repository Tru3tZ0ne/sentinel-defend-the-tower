Implemented gameplay contract:
1. Cyber Command uses the existing NDR product ID. Its four side emitters produce
   two horizontal damage strips, with control links identifying their source.
   NDR enables tracking for operator, EDR, MDR and temporary-team projectiles,
   reveals/marks threats, and adds 10% MDR damage per NDR level. Homing preserves
   speed, retargets dead/already-hit targets and has a 2.4-second flight lifetime.
2. All deployed product locations feed colored energy into the XDR appliance.
   Charge lasts 0.65 seconds, then XDR itself emits the main targeted laser.
   Multiple EDR turrets and both NGAF posts have separate links. Thin links do
   not deal damage; the large laser uses the same angled segment for rendering
   and collision. Sensors and connected product types strengthen its damage.
   Correlation death chains require actual XDR ownership and two sensors.
3. The accessible event campaign starts with 10,000 HP, EDR/NDR/XDR level 1,
   and exactly three bosses: ransomware at 25s, botnet at 60s, siege at 95s.
   No fourth boss. Each boss attacks every four seconds. Explicit allied support
   begins after 12 seconds and deals real damage, ensuring a real death within
   about 20 seconds of entry. Only deaths count as cleared bosses. Emergency
   cover preserves 1 HP. Start instructions and results disclose both assists.
   At 120 active seconds, after all three deaths, show the completion result.
4. Upgrades do not pause. Players click/tap or press 1/2/3; after three seconds
   the marked recommendation deploys. Movement and Q/E/R remain active. An
   explicit pause freezes combat and offer timers. Keep the compact mobile dock.
   The frame loop uses <=1/60s physics substeps and <=0.25s catch-up per frame;
   do not restore a 50ms clamp that slows the campaign on lower frame rates.
5. Rank solely by remaining integer HP, capped at 10,000. Ties share a rank.
   Keep the new local namespaces sentinel.stand.health.scores.v1 and
   sentinel.stand.health.best.v1, preserving old score records. The optional
   shared endpoint is window.__STAND_HEALTH_API, with its own backend namespace.
   The published default is local-only: do not imply cross-computer ranking.
   Guard asynchronous result submissions so an old result cannot replace replay.
