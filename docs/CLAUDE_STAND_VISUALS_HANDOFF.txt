Current gameplay contract:
1. The campaign starts with 10,000 HP and EDR/MDR/HCI level 1. It ends after
   120 active seconds, or earlier on zero HP or business availability. Exactly
   three bosses enter at 25/60/95 seconds. Only actual boss deaths count.
   All three must die by the deadline to win. Do not restore mandatory allied
   damage, a one-HP floor, fabricated deaths or guaranteed clears.
2. CC uses the NDR ID. It retains horizontal side laser strips and its existing
   MDR boost. MDR remains unchanged: CC-only tracking, existing damage/rate,
   10% damage per CC level and fast turn response. Ordinary operator/EDR/service
   bullets need BOTH CC and XDR, with limited forward-cone guidance, range and
   slower turning. Preserve finite flight life, constant speed and safe
   retargeting after a kill or pierced target. Aiming and movement must matter.
3. XDR visibly collects energy from every deployed device, including AI and
   Agent Builder, then fires the main laser from the XDR appliance. Thin links
   deal no damage; beam collision follows its actual angled segment. The large
   official XDR mark/name must stay legible on desktop and phone during firing.
   Death-chain branching/damage are deliberately restrained to avoid idle clears.
4. Boss strikes warn a fixed column before damage. Moving out avoids the hit;
   AI extends warning time slightly. Support cooldown/aim timing must matter.
   Incident Response deals 20% max HP to bosses and 60% to ordinary threats,
   retains its minimum damage, heals and grants a hit shield. Keep Q/E/R.
5. Cloud is a real tradeoff. HCI supplies compute and fire-rate support, aStor
   adds shielding and some capacity, AI produces scored work and Agent Builder
   adds workflow output and modest automation. AI offers require HCI; Agent
   offers require HCI and AI. Demand above capacity reduces availability and
   delivered output. Cloud-only investment must remain vulnerable to attacks.
6. Score actual delivered work, not purchases. Show remaining HP, HCI/business,
   AI and Agent Builder components (0-10,000 each). Total weights are 55/25/12/8.
   Only victories rank. Ties share rank. Preserve earlier boards; use
   sentinel.stand.enterprise.scores.v1, sentinel.stand.enterprise.best.v1 and
   optional window.__STAND_ENTERPRISE_API with its own backend namespace.
   GitHub Pages defaults to browser-local ranking; never imply a global board.
7. Upgrades are nonblocking 1/2/3 or tap, with a three-second auto-recommendation.
   Explicit pause freezes production, combat, skills and offer timers. Terminal
   states cannot award later work or allow upgrades/abilities. Guard stale async
   score submissions so a previous result cannot overwrite a replay.
