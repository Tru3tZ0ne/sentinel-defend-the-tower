Visual contract:
1. One basic robot, 10,000 HP and no preinstalled products. The opening offer
   is EDR/MDR/HCI, with 1/2/3, tap and the three-second recommendation.
2. Mount products on ONE mech: EDR shoulder cannons, MDR gun arm, NGAF shield,
   CC head sensor, HCI compute pod, XDR backpack, aStor hip storage, IAG leg
   module, AI compute and Agent Builder wrist console. Do not restore a
   growing field of permanent product appliances.
3. Total purchased levels 0/3/7/14 select armor stages I/II/III/IV. Replay
   returns to a plain robot while retaining cached geometry. Keep upgrades
   physically visible without letting the mech obscure combat.
4. Reuse official Sangfor marks. XDR has a compact backpack mark and readable
   XDR plate, not an oversized floating board. Models are original arcade
   concepts, not certified commercial appliances. AI uses AICP; Agent Builder
   has a functional label, not a fabricated official logo.
5. Phone choices sit bottom-right, clear of support buttons/HP and below the
   forward enemy view. Check toasts/card overlap and full-mech framing at both
   movement extremes, short portraits and in both languages.

Combat contract:
1. Start at 10,000 HP. End at 120 active seconds, or earlier at zero HP/service
   availability. Exactly three bosses enter at 25/60/95. All must actually die
   by the deadline. No guaranteed clear, HP floor, fabricated death or forced
   allied damage. Only victories rank.
2. CC uses NDR internally. Retain side laser strips and its MDR boost. MDR
   retains CC-only tracking, existing damage/rate and fast turning. Ordinary
   operator/EDR/service shots need BOTH CC and XDR, with limited range/cone,
   slower turning, constant speed and finite lifetime. Retarget safely after
   kills/piercing; aiming and movement must matter.
3. All owned modules charge XDR, including AI and Agent Builder. Links are
   visual only. XDR alone fires the damaging beam from the moving backpack;
   its target stays committed. Collision follows the actual angled segment.
   Keep restrained chain damage so idle play can fail.
4. Boss attacks warn a fixed column before damage; moving out avoids it.
   AI extends warnings slightly. Preserve Q/E/R timing and support tradeoffs.
5. HCI supplies compute/business throughput and fire-rate support. aStor adds
   snapshots; AI produces work; Agent Builder adds workflows and modest
   automation. AI requires HCI; Agent offers require HCI + AI. Overload reduces
   availability/output. Cloud-only investment must lose to uncontained attacks.
6. Score actual delivered work and HP, not purchases. Four components range
   0-10,000 and use HP/business/AI/agents weights 55/25/12/8. Preserve tie ranks,
   old boards and enterprise storage/API keys. Pages is browser-local ranking
   unless a separate backend is configured. Do not claim a global board.
7. Choices never pause combat. Explicit pause freezes combat/work/support/
   offers. Terminal states award no later work/upgrades. Guard stale async
   result submissions so they cannot overwrite replay screens.

Balance:
- Aim roughly 60-70% clears for a reasonable casual mixed-build policy, not a
  security spending quota or guaranteed first-time-player rate.
- Read current seeded and held-out results in the validation record. The probe
  compiles production combat closures, not a simplified replacement engine.
- After real combat changes, run both viewports and held-out seeds; record
  loadouts, deaths, time, support, HP and service work. Do not select favorable
  runs. Human playtesting on actual event hardware remains a follow-up.

Performance:
- Preserve geometry, antialiasing, shadows, bloom, lights, animation, particles,
  lightning, text and native DPR up to 2. Do not cut effects/entities/resolution
  to disguise cost. Do not add external runtime dependencies.
- Retain instanced threats/bullets and the order-preserving collision grid.
  Worm children born during the same piercing shot must remain hittable.
- Retain object pools, stable compaction, shared projection/lightning buffers,
  cached modules and pooled support teams. Pool limits apply only to recycled
  objects; never discard still-live effects. Replays must reuse models.
- Fixed fixture: ten maxed products, support teams, 64 threats, 256 shots, CC
  strips and XDR link/beam = 333 draws / 491,226 triangles / 112 geometries.
  Older separate appliances were 440 draws / 526,724 triangles. This compares
  scene cost, not identical geometry or a universal FPS improvement percentage.
- Bullets use one draw; growth to capacity 1,024 retains shared geometry.
  Keep the existing <450 draw budget and verify native DPR 2.
- Fixed counts clear transient FX for reproducibility. A separate FX fixture
  retains 3,000 particles/80 texts/24 lightning effects. Neither establishes
  natural-play smoothness. Use the active recorder with effects untouched and
  identify the renderer before timing claims.
- Preserve <=1/60s substeps and <=0.25s catch-up per rendered frame.
